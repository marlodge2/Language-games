HOPALONG

a reading pacer — by John and Muriel Higgins, 1988–1992, revised 2026

HOPALONG runs a highlight through a text at a speed you control, to help you find a comfortable reading rhythm and see how it changes across a passage. It is not a race or a test — it's a way to notice your own reading habits, ideally to discuss with a teacher afterwards.
