Copy of a notice posted around the town of Split in 1982

TURISTICKI SAVEZ SPLITA

WELCOME IN SPLIT on which door knock all the world. On the intersection of two worlds, of the West and the Orient, here you can find the monuments of Greek, Roman and Slav culture. The centouries have made here in stone and marble beauty and harmony. Each part of old town presents the expert deal in which anyone can find estetic, refine and urbanistic sense of the generation who had made it. Diocletian palate is declared as the composit part of the world culture hertiage because of exceptional beauty and architectonic harmony.

WELCOME IN SPLIT on the autobus meeting international and international regatta "Kup Dalmacije" where five thousand guests and contests from whole Europe and World, about hundred journalists and camermans are presented, and in which tourist workers, journalists and yachtsmans from England and Jugoslavia have the most important rules.

WELCOME IN SPLIT, the town of great sport tradition. 1979, Split was organizator and the host of Eight Mediterranean games, and 1981, it organized Europe Championship in water-polo and swimming, and at same time it organized many other international and national sport contests. From his reach sport history Split has numerous trophies.

WELCOME IN SPLIT on semifinal match of UEFA CUP, which will be hold on Wensday, on 11th of April amoung HAJDUK and TOOTENHAM. Your favorites, players of "Tootenham" expect from all fer and correct cheer. Let we permit on this match win versed, clever and best rival.

Split and his inhabitans expect you with open harts in convicttion that this match will flow in keeping company and freendship.

We hope in Split you feel as at your home.
