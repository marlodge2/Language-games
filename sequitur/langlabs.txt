On language laboratories
The language laboratory used to be 
the great status symbol
of the language teaching profession.
Every school which could afford one 
had to have one
or risk being thought behind the times.
But the hoped-for results never came.
Language laboratories were left unrepaired,
sold off, or simply ignored.
It is worth asking why.
Perhaps one reason was 
the fear of complex machinery,
symbolised in Goethe's story 
of the Sorcerer's Apprentice.
The apprentice in the story 
used his master's magic broom 
to do the housework,
but could not remember the magic words 
to switch the thing off.
In the same way teachers sometimes feared
the humiliation of not being able 
to control the machine
if they should attempt to use it.
