Nasreddin's Shirt
One day Nasreddin came to a village
where there had been no rain for many weeks.
The villagers welcomed him.
'Hoja, you are a clever man.
Make the rain come.'
Nasreddin agreed
and asked the villagers to bring him
all the water they had.
They brought buckets and bottles out of their houses.
Nasreddin poured all this water 
into a drinking trough in the town square.
Then he took off his shirt and began to wash it.
The villagers were furious at this waste
but he ignored them.
Suddenly there was a flash of lightning
And a clap of thunder 
And it began to pour with rain.
Everybody was amazed.
'It's always like this,' said Nasreddin.
'I only have one shirt
And whenever I need to dry it, it rains.'
