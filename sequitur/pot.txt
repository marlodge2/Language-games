Nasreddin and the Pot
One day Nasreddin borrowed a cooking pot
from his neighbour Ali.
The next day
he returned it
with another little pot inside. 
‘That’s not mine!’ said Ali.
‘Yes, it is,” said Nasreddin.
‘While your pot was staying with me
it had a baby,’
A few days later
Nasreddin asked to borrow the pot again.
Ali agreed, and waited
to see if he would again 
be given two pots.
But time passed and Ali grew impatient
and went to demand 
the return of his pot.
‘I am sorry,’ said Nasreddin.
'I am afraid your pot died.'
'Died!' exclaimed Ali.
'How can a metal pot die?'
'Well,' Nasreddin answered, 
‘you believed me 
when I said your pot had had a baby!’
