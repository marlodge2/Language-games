Office boy in London
The head of the department 
was a character named Lee.
His office rigout was a silk top hat 
and a frock coat.
He got to the office at 8 a m
and personally opened all the letters,
marking a number of them PSM in blue pencil.
If you got one of the marked ones 
you had to "Please See Me".
He didn't miss much of what was going on.
At 10:30 to the minute every day
he emerged from his glass-windowed office,
put on his top hat,
and made his way 
to one of the well-known Fleet Street pubs
for his daily glass of champagne.
The old chap must have had a few good friends
and he often found it necessary 
to send a note to his wife
saying he would be home late that night.
I was sometimes chosen 
to be one of the note-carriers.
His wife would invite the note-carrier in
and, after making him swear
he would not divulge it to her husband,
offer him a glass of port.
When the messenger returned to the office
to report the safe delivery of the note,
Lee would ask, 
"Did she give you a glass of wine?"
On being told "No", 
he would say "She's very mean.
I must tell her next time 
you are to have one."
