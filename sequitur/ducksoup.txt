Nasreddin's visitors
One day a visitor came
to Nasreddin’s house.
“I am your cousin from Konya,”
he said,
and I have brought you a duck
to celebrate the visit.”
Nasreddin was delighted.
He asked his wife
to cook the duck 
and gave the visitor
a fine meal.
The next day another visitor came.
“I am a friend of the man
who brought you the duck,” 
he said.
Nasreddin invited him in
and gave him a good meal.
Then another visitor came
and said he was a friend of the friend
of the man 
who had brought the duck.
Again Nasreddin invited him in
but he was getting suspicious.
People were using his house 
as a free restaurant.
Then another man came
and said he was a friend of the friend of the friend
of the man who brought the duck.
Nasreddin invited him in for supper
and his wife brought soup to the table.
“What kind of soup is this?”
asked the visitor
when he tasted it.
“It tastes just like warm water.”
“Ah,” said Nasreddin.
“That is the soup of the soup
of the soup of the duck.”
