Seesaw
a game for language learners
by John Higgins, 2026

You see six children and know the weights of one, two or three of them.
Click a child to put them on the seesaw — click again to move them to the other side, and a third time to take them off. The seesaw reacts as soon as you place someone.
Use the results to deduce the hidden weights.
Enter your guesses below each child.
Remember that a little more/less means that the difference is 1 to 3 kilos,
more/less means 4 to 6 kilos, and a lot more/less means 7 or more kilos.
