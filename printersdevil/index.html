Printer's Devil

A word game by John Higgins — a 2026 revival of a program originally written for language learners in the 1980s
A printer's devil was the mischievous apprentice who was blamed for every misprint. Here, the devil has mutilated five words in exactly the same way — perhaps by reversing them, swapping letters around, or dropping the vowels. Study the mutilated words, work out what has been done to them, then pick the correct rule from the choices offered.
