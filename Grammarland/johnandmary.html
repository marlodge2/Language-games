ภาษาไทย (Thai instructions)

What can I say?
  • Move someone (or something) — Put John in the kitchen, Send Rex to the garden, Bring Mary into the living room (bring only makes sense for moving into the house, not out to the garden)
  • Work a door — there are two now, so say which: Open the kitchen door (between the kitchen and the living room), Close the garden door (between the living room and the garden)
  • Nobody can walk through a closed door — if you try, you'll be told which one to open first
  • Ask where someone is — Where is John?, Where's Rex? — or where two or three of them are at once — Where are John and Mary?
  • Or ask who's in a room — Who is in the garden? — it'll say Nobody. if it's empty
  • Ask about a room or a door — Is Mary in the kitchen?, Are Mary and Rex in the garden?, Is the kitchen door open?
  • Ask if two or three of them are together — Are John and Mary together?, Are John and Mary and Rex together?
  • Ask about sight lines — Can John see Mary?, Can John see Rex? — true if they share a room, or if every door between their rooms is open (the kitchen and the garden aren't next to each other, so seeing between them needs both doors open)
  • Or just tell it something — John is in the garden., The kitchen door is open., Rex can see Mary. — and it'll agree, or correct you if you've got it wrong
  • Rex is a dog, so he takes it, not he/she — that's on purpose
  • Leave the box empty and press Enter to let the computer get on with things by itself — it'll ask itself a question and answer it on your next empty Enter, or just wander someone to a new room or work a door
