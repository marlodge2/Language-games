DROPIN

A sentence puzzle for EFL learners — adapted from QUOTEFALL by John and Muriel Higgins, 1995, revised 2026.

Every letter in the grid has slid down its column into the wrong row. Click a letter, then click another letter in the same column to swap them, until every row reads correctly. Dark squares mark the end of a word; punctuation is filled in for you. The computer picks the text at random — if you get stuck, Peek shows it for five seconds.
Add your own text

Type a sentence and it joins the pool the computer picks puzzles from (and loads straight away so you can try it). Needs to be 40–120 characters (roughly 8–22 words).
