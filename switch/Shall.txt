HOW, AT THE AGE OF 25, I SHALL FIND TRUE HAPPINESS
HOW, AT THE AGE OF 25, I WILL FIND TRUE HAPPINESS
HOW, AT THE AGE OF 25, I AM GOING TO FIND TRUE HAPPINESS
----------------------------
#
#
--------
#
#
Somewhere lives a pretty girl I shall marry.
Somewhere lives a pretty girl I will marry.
Somewhere lives a pretty girl I am going to marry.
Somewhere stands the church where we will be photographed
Somewhere stands the church where we shall be photographed
#
with all our relatives and great Aunt Ada.
#
#
Somewhere is the bank which will give me my mortgage.
Somewhere is the bank which shall give me my mortgage.
Somewhere is the bank which is going to give me my mortgage.
Somewhere is the coastal town,
#
#
where we will go for our Sunday drives
where we shall go for our Sunday drives
where we are going to go for our Sunday drives
in the middle of winter.
#
#
Somewhere when this happens, I shall take out my brain,
Somewhere when this happens, I will take out my brain,
#
solemnly burn it, and read a funeral service over it,
#
#
for I will no longer need it.  
for I shall no longer need it. 
for I am no longer going to need it.
(Paul Sutton, 16)
#
#
