Going to market
One day Nasreddin set off to market with his son, leading his donkey. They passed some people who asked, "Why are you walking when you have a donkey you could ride?"
So Nasreddin mounted the donkey. Now they passed some people who said: "Look at that cruel man who rides while his boy has to walk."
Nasreddin dismounted and put the boy on the donkey. Now the onlookers said "Why does the boy ride while his old father has to walk?"
So Nasreddin got up in front of the boy and they rode together. Now the onlookers said: "How cruel they are! The poor donkey cannot carry such a big weight."
They took pity on the donkey and picked it up and carried it. But now the onlookers laughed at them.
