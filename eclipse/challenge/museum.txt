At the Museum
When Nasreddin visited Istanbul, he liked to go to the Topkapi Museum. Once he walked round with a group of tourists who were listening to a guide. The guide stopped in front of a case with a crown covered in jewels.
"This crown was used for the coronation of the emperor six hundred years ago," said the guide. 
"No, it was six hundred and four years ago," said Nasreddin.
The guide ignored him and moved on to a case containing a sword. "This sword was used to execute the rebel leaders four hundred and fifty years ago," he said.
"No," said Nasreddin. "Four hundred and fifty-four years ago." 
The guide was angry. "How is it that you know these dates so accurately?" he asked.
"When I came here last time, you said the coronation happened six hundred years ago and the rebels were executed four hundred and fifty years ago," Nasreddin answered. "And that was exactly four years ago."
###
The Topkapi Museum is in Istanbul beside the Bosphorus.
