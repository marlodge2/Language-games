Welcome to ECLIPSE

A text rebuilding game by John and Muriel Higgins
July 2026

This version is COMPETITION mode, where two players take turns trying to find the words of the text. Correct or wrong, a guess passes the turn to the other player — unless the word has already been found, in which case you get another go. Whoever scores more when the text is complete wins.
