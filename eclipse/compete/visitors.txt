Nasreddin's Visitors
One day a man came to Nasreddin's house. "I am your cousin from Konya," he said "and I have brought you a duck to celebrate the visit."
Nasreddin was delighted. His wife cooked the duck and they enjoyed a good meal.
The next day a man came and said he was a friend of the man  who had brought the duck. Nasreddin invited him in and gave  him a good dinner.
Then another man came and said he was a friend of the friend of the man who had brought the duck. By now Nasreddin was becoming suspicious, but he invited him in anyway. 
Then another man came and said he was a friend of the friend of the friend of the man who had brought the duck.
Nasreddin invited him to have dinner with him and his wife brought some  soup to the table.
The visitor tasted it and said, "What kind of soup is this? It tastes like warm water."
"Ah," said Nasreddin. "It is the soup of the soup of the soup of the duck."
###
The first visitor comes from Konya, a town in the south of Turkey.
