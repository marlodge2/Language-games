Nasreddin borrows a pot
One day Nasreddin borrowed a pot from his neighbour Ali. The next day he returned it with another little pot inside. 
"That's not mine", said Ali. 
"Yes it is", said Nasreddin. "While your pot was staying with me, it had a baby." 
One week later Nasreddin asked Ali to lend him the pot again. Ali agreed and waited to see if Nasreddin would again give him back two pots for one. However, a week passed and still Nasreddin had not brought the pot back. Eventually Ali lost patience and went to demand his property. 
"I am afraid your pot died", said Nasreddin. 
"Died!" said Ali, "How can a pot die?" 
"Well you believed me when I told you that your pot had had a baby", said Nasreddin.
###
Nasreddin's neighbour is called Ali.
