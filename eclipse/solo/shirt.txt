Nasreddin's shirt
One day Nasreddin came to a village where there had been no rain for many weeks. The villagers crowded round him and said "Sir, you are a clever man. Make the rain come."
Nasreddin agreed and told the villagers to bring him all the water they had. One after another they brought him the precious jars of water which they had been saving. He poured it all into the drinking trough in the market square. Then he took off his shirt and began to wash it. The villagers were furious at this waste, but he ignored them and continued washing his shirt. 
Suddenly there was a clap of thunder, and it began to rain. All the villagers were amazed at this miracle and asked him how he had done it.
"I knew this would happen," said Nasreddin. "I only have one shirt, and whenever I want to dry it, it rains."
###
This is a modern variant of a traditional Turkish folk tale told about Nasreddin Hoja. Nasreddin is the hero of many stories. Hoja means wise man, and nobody can be sure whether Nasreddin is very clever or very stupid.
