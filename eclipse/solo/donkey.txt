Nareddin's donkey
Nasreddin's neighbour needed to take some produce to market, so he asked Nasreddin if he could borrow his donkey for a day. Nasreddin did not feel like lending his animal, so he said: "I am sorry. I have lent it to somebody else."
Just then the donkey brayed very loudly from behind a wall.
"But your donkey is still here", said the neighbour. "I can hear it from over the wall."
Nasreddin was indignant. "Who will you believe?" he asked. "Your Mullah or a donkey?"

###
A mullah is an Islamic religious leader.
