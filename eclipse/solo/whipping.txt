Nasreddin's Punishment
The Emir of Nasreddin's country allowed the citizens to come to his court one day every month to ask for help or make a complaint.
One day Nasreddin arrived and was admitted. "What do you want from me?" asked the Emir.
"I want you to stop being a cruel tyrant," said Nasreddin.
"How dare you insult me!" said the Emir. "Guards, give this man fifty lashes."
The guards brought out their whips and started the beating. After twenty-five strokes Nasreddin held up his hand and said: "Stop! You must give the rest of this punishment to your steward."
"Why?" asked the Emir.
"Because he said he would let me in to see you only if I gave him half of what you gave me."
