A Doctor's Day
The first patient at morning surgery was a small boy, brought in by his mother. He had been coughing all night and had a slight temperature. The doctor prescribed some pills and told the boy's mother to keep him out of school for a few days.
Next came a woman who had scalded herself the week before. The doctor was glad to see when he changed the dressing that the injury was healing well. He told the woman to come back in a week and to take things easy meanwhile.
The last patient was a farmer who had strained his back digging. The doctor gave him some ointment and told him to spend a few days in bed. In fact he told everybody to rest except himself.
