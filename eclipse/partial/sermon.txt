Nareddin's sermons
The villagers asked Nasreddin to preach the Friday sermon at the local mosque. Nasreddin agreed. He began his first sermon by asking "Do you know what I am going to say?" The villagers all said No. He continued: "You are not clever enough to understand me", and he went home.
The next Friday he began: "Do you know what I am going to say?" This time the villagers were ready and they all said Yes. "So you don't need me to tell you", said Nasreddin, and he went home.
The next Friday Nasreddin began his sermon again with the words "Do you know what I am going to tell you?" 
This time the villagers thought they knew the right answer. They answered: "Some of us know, and some of us do not know."
Nasreddin said: "Well, let the ones who know tell the ones who do not know." And then he went home.

###

Nasreddin will probably not be asked to preach in that mosque again.
