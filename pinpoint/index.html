PINPOINT

A text game by John and Muriel Higgins
revised Summer, 2026

You see a text which has been masked out apart from one word. Above it are eight titles to choose from. Which one is right? If you guess right you win 200 points, but each wrong guess costs 50 points.

You can ask to see more text by opening the window wider, three words, five words, seven ... But each pair of extra words cost 10 points. See how long it takes you to reach 1000 points!
