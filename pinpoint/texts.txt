French Toast
Whisk two eggs with a cup of milk, one teaspoon vanilla, one teaspoon sugar, and a pinch of cinnamon and salt in a shallow bowl. Heat a little butter in a frying pan over medium heat. Dip four slices of slightly stale bread into the egg mixture, coating both sides but not soaking too long. Fry each slice for two to three minutes per side until golden brown and crisp at the edges.

Bernard Shaw and Winston Churchill
Bernard Shaw and Winston Churchill did not get on well. Shaw once sent Churchill two tickets for the opening night of one of his plays. He enclosed a note which said: 'Bring a friend (if you have one).' Churchill sent back the tickets with a note that said: 'I will be busy that night. Send tickets for the second night (if there is one).'

On language laboratories
The language laboratory used to be the great status symbol of the language teaching profession. But the hoped-for results never came. Was this due to the fear of complex machinery, symbolised in Goethe's story of the Sorcerer's Apprentice. The apprentice in the story used his master's magic broom to do the housework, but could not remember the magic words to switch the thing off. In the same way teachers sometimes feared the humiliation of not being able to control the machine.
